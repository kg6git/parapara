Parapara

パラパラ — the sound of flipping pages

Speed-read any page right where it is. Press one shortcut and a small card floats over the article, showing the text a few words at a time.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

What it does

Never lose your spot

The sentence you are reading lights up in the original page. Pause and keep reading normally.

Built for technical reading

Stops before code blocks, tables and math instead of flattening them into a word stream.

No selecting, no right-click

Press Alt+V (Option+V on macOS) to outline what you can read, then start from any paragraph.

Private by design

Everything runs on your computer. No analytics, no ads, no account.
